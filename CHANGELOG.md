# Changelog

## 1.5.1
- Owner account setup now asks for a name before the PIN, with an optional email kept as a record on the phone.
- The lock screen greets the owner by name and shows who owns the device under the PIN field.
- Staff get a name too, so the app can say who is signed in.
- Corrected NYSC everywhere (the earlier build said NTSC).

## 1.5.0
- Six design categories: African attire, office and corporate, bridal and occasion, casual and everyday, men's wear, kids and family.
- Category filters sit alongside the existing Studio, Client refs and AI drafts filters, and the two combine.
- A 29-style starter library covering Bubu, Ankara two-pieces, kaftan, agbada, senator, sheath dresses, corset and mermaid, jumpsuits, men's kaftan and safari, and kids' sets.
- Any design can be moved to another category from its detail sheet.
- Fixed: saving a design failed when no AI key was configured, because the form read a field that only exists with a key.
- Fixed: toasts no longer block taps on the button underneath.

## 1.4.0
- Branded welcome cover on launch, using the studio splash artwork.
- PIN login sits on the cover, with shortcut buttons for Gallery, Clients, Jobs and Money. Each shortcut still requires the PIN, and a staff PIN cannot open Money.
- Matching native Android splash so the artwork shows before the app finishes loading.

## 1.3.0
- Owner PIN login with salted hash, first-run PIN creation and auto lock after idle.
- Staff PIN role: jobs, clients, designs and deliveries only. No money screens, no settings.
- Padlock in the header to lock on demand.

## 1.2.0
- Bank transfer details pre-filled in Settings, printed on receipts and payment messages.

## 1.1.0
- Gallery with Studio, Client refs and AI draft filters.
- Photo upload from camera or album, compressed on device.
- Per-client design library on the client card.
- Tailoring guide: fabric yardage, cut list, construction steps.

## 1.0.0
- First build: clients, measurements, jobs, money, deliveries, training, WhatsApp reminders, client request form.
